.01About

I’m a postdoctoral researcher working on responsible AI for decentralized systems: machine learning that protects privacy, explains its decisions and keeps its energy footprint in check.

My research spans privacy-preserving and decentralized machine learning, explainable AI, energy-aware AI and, more recently, foundation models and generative and agentic AI. I apply it to healthcare, telecommunications, energy and mobility, and I teach software engineering at Democritus University of Thrace.

I hold an Integrated Master of Engineering (2022, honors) and a PhD (2025, GPA 10/10) in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Democritus University of Thrace, both advised by Pavlos S. Efraimidis. My dissertation, Optimizing Responsible Decentralized Machine Learning Methods for AI Applications, developed machine learning methods that bring privacy, explainability and sustainability together.

During my PhD I worked on the FLAMENCO project for privacy-preserving diagnosis of communication disorders in children, and was lead researcher of FLORA at ATHENA Research Center, a privacy-aware ovulation-tracking app. I spent Erasmus+ research stays at CTTC in Barcelona, studying the energy efficiency of decentralized learning in telecom networks, and at Wageningen University & Research, on privacy-preserving analytics for agri-food systems.

Since October 2026 I am a postdoctoral researcher at DUTH on responsible AI for decentralized systems and foundation models. I also teach Software Engineering at the Department of Management Science & Technology in Kavala, and serve as COO and researcher at Indigma, a European startup building responsible AI solutions, where I lead research on generative and agentic AI, digital twins and AI-enabled decision support.

Current research

Responsible Artificial Intelligence for Decentralized Systems and Foundation Models: Privacy, Explainability and Sustainability

How can we train useful models on data that must stay where it is, explain what they learned, and do it without wasting energy? My research builds and evaluates AI methods, from decentralized learning to foundation models, that answer these three questions together, and tests them in real applications.

Research interests
  • Responsible AI
  • Explainable AI
  • Privacy-Preserving ML
  • Sustainable & Green AI
  • Generative & Agentic AI
  • Foundation Models
  • Digital Twins
  • Decentralized & Edge AI
  • Time-Series Forecasting
  • AI for Healthcare
  • AI for Energy & Telecoms
Three pillars

Privacy

Health, energy and telecom data often can’t be pooled in one place. I work on privacy-preserving and decentralized learning, coping with scarce and heterogeneous data, and protections such as ledger-based authentication.

Explainability

Models that make decisions about patients or power grids should show their reasoning. I work on explainable ML for high-stakes settings, from breast cancer classification to non-technical loss detection in power systems.

Sustainability

Training has an energy and carbon cost. I measure it and design around it: energy-aware traffic prediction for cellular networks, carbon-aware forecasting with spiking neural networks and bio-inspired models.
